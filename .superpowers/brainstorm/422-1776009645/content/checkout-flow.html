Checkout & order tracking — how should the payment flow feel?

From cart to confirmed order. This is where trust and clarity matter most.

A

Single-Page Checkout

Everything on one page: delivery address (with saved addresses dropdown), order summary, delivery time estimate, payment button. Stripe redirects externally for payment, then returns to a confirmation page with order timeline tracker. Simple, fast, minimal clicks.

B

Multi-Step Wizard

Step 1: Confirm address → Step 2: Review order & add notes → Step 3: Payment. Progress bar at top. Each step is focused and uncluttered. After payment: animated confirmation with confetti, then live order timeline with status updates. More guided but more clicks.

C

Bottom Sheet Checkout (Mobile-First)

Checkout slides up as a bottom sheet over the restaurant page. Address, summary, and pay button in a compact scrollable sheet. On desktop it becomes a modal/drawer. After payment: order tracking page with real-time status bar, map with driver location, and ETA countdown. Feels seamless — never leaves the browsing context.
