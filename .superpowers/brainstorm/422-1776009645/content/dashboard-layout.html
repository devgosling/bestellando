Restaurant dashboard — how should owners manage their business?

This is where restaurant owners handle incoming orders, menu, and settings.

A

Sidebar Navigation Dashboard

Classic admin layout: fixed left sidebar with nav links (Übersicht, Bestellungen, Speisekarte, Öffnungszeiten, Einstellungen). Main content area on the right. Top bar shows restaurant name, online/offline toggle, and notification bell. Incoming orders appear as cards with accept/reject buttons. Familiar pattern, works well on desktop.

B

Tab-Based Dashboard

Horizontal tab bar at top: Orders | Menu | Hours | Settings. No sidebar — full width for content. Orders tab is the default landing with a Kanban-like board (Neu → In Zubereitung → Bereit → Abgeholt). Big online/offline toggle front and center. Simpler, mobile-friendly, less admin-feeling.

C

Order-First Dashboard

Orders dominate the screen — live feed of incoming orders with sound notifications. Secondary pages (menu, hours, settings) accessible via a compact icon bar or hamburger menu. Think of a kitchen display system. Optimized for the primary task: handling orders fast. Other management tasks are secondary.
