What design direction feels right for Bestellando?

Pick the overall aesthetic and personality. This drives all visual decisions downstream.

A

Bold & Playful (Uber Eats / Wolt style)

Bright orange/yellow accent, rounded cards, playful micro-animations, emoji-friendly, large food photography, fun hover effects. Feels casual and appetizing. Target: young urban audience.

B

Clean & Premium (Deliveroo / DoorDash style)

Teal/mint accent, generous whitespace, sophisticated typography, subtle shadows, glass-morphism cards, minimal but elegant. Feels trustworthy and premium. Target: quality-conscious customers.

C

Warm & Inviting (Lieferando / Just Eat style)

Deep orange-red accent, warm tones, friendly illustrations, comfortable spacing, familiar patterns. Feels like home. Emphasis on local restaurants and community feel. Target: broad audience, all ages.
