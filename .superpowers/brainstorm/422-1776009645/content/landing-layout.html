Landing page layout — what draws users in?

The first thing visitors see. Sets the tone for the whole experience.

A

Full-Screen Hero with Search

Large food photography fills the viewport. A centered search bar with address input sits on top. Below: category chips, popular restaurants grid, and "how it works" section. Similar to current layout but polished.

B

Split Hero — Search Left, Illustration Right

Left half: headline, subtitle, address search bar, category quick-links. Right half: warm illustrated graphic of food delivery scene (or collage of food photos). Below fold: restaurant cards, testimonials, app download. Clean separation of action and visual.

C

Compact Header + Immediate Content

Small hero banner with search bar (not full viewport). Immediately shows restaurant categories as scrollable chip row, followed by restaurant cards grid. Gets users to browsing faster — less "marketing", more app-like. Think Lieferando's approach.
